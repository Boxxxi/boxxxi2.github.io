08✦AI Models

Dynamo shows exactly when splitting vision encoding from LLM serving pays off

NVIDIA’s encode-prefill-decode guide reports up to 5× faster time to first token and 7× faster end-to-end response for the right multimodal mix, then maps the failure cases. In a ten-image workload, colocated encoding cut first-token latency 58%; longer outputs, large dense models and homogeneous dedicated encoder GPUs shrink or erase the advantage.

WHY: Multimodal serving · open Dynamo workflow · honest boundary conditions
