02✦AI Models

THE BIG SIGNAL

IBM makes Spyre a first-class PyTorch backend instead of a bespoke model port

IBM's torch-spyre path now compiles ordinary PyTorch through TorchDynamo and Inductor, connects into vLLM and llm-d, and cuts its custom compiler surface by roughly sevenfold. Published tests report up to 1.7× faster prefill and 2.4× faster decode after moving graph work off the compute path, plus hardware-agnostic vLLM layers within 3.4% of native H100 throughput.

IBM Research ↗Oct 26Primary
WHY: AI infrastructure · alternative accelerators · native PyTorch · measured portability
03✦AI Models

A 325M model trains from scratch on an inference-only NPU—and matches its B200 twin

FINAL-Bench trained identical 325.6M-parameter byte models on a commercial inference NPU and a B200 using the same initialization, data and schedule. On 1,024 unseen windows the losses were 0.7993 and 0.7994; both weights and the evaluation script are public, though the stability technique remains undisclosed pending a patent.

FINAL-Bench · Hugging Face ↗Oct 26Primary
WHY: AI hardware · reproducible comparison · open weights · inference silicon

Storage webhooks can now trigger Jobs with scoped secrets, making the release especially practical for repeatable agent pipelines.

PINNED

Cosmos3-H-Dreams turns surgical robot motion into a live generative simulator

NVIDIA's open research release conditions a 16B world model on 28 dimensions of Versius robot kinematics, then distills it into two- and four-step students that run interactively on one RTX PRO 6000. The code, checkpoints and adaptation recipe are public; the team explicitly frames visual realism as a starting point, not proof of biomechanical or clinical validity.

NVIDIA · Hugging Face ↗Oct 20Primary
