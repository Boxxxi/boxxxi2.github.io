01⌬RoboticsMIT · courtesy of the researchers

THE BIG SIGNAL

MIT's flapping-wing microrobot now carries its own sensors and computer, and lands on a sunflower

Kevin Chen's Soft and Micro Robotics Lab, with Jonathan How's group and Cornell's Elizabeth Farrell Helbling, cut the motion-capture tether from their four-winged, paperclip-weight flier. The team made the soft artificial-muscle actuators 50% larger while raising net lift 46%, for a lift-to-weight ratio of 1.9, enough to carry a 244 mg package with an IMU, an optical-flow sensor, a time-of-flight height sensor and a microcontroller. A deliberately simplified estimator that updates position faster, rather than more accurately, kept drift low: hovering and point-to-point flights stayed within a few centimeters, and a joystick-guided run landed on a sunflower outside the lab. Power still comes over a wire. The paper is in Science Robotics.

WHY: Insect-scale aerial robots · onboard state estimation · soft actuators · Science Robotics
02⌬RoboticsRemedy Robotics

Remedy Robotics wants a stroke surgeon to steer a catheter from another city

A long IEEE Spectrum feature on Remedy's N1, a robot for thrombectomy, the clot-removal operation for ischemic stroke that only about 1 in 10 eligible patients receive in time. Instead of a guide wire, a robotic arm advances a steerable catheter whose tip is pulled by tiny wires; markings on the catheter let a computer-vision tracker correct for its twist, so 'right' on the X-ray means right, and a CT overlay shows the vessels X-ray can't. The system halts all motion when round-trip latency passes 150 ms. In September 2025 a Toronto neurosurgeon used it on ten consenting non-emergency patients, six from an adjoining room, two from elsewhere in the hospital and two from a hospital across town. No telesurgery system has full regulatory approval, and no US patient has been operated on remotely.

03✦AI ModelsGoogle DeepMind

Genie 4 lets you rewrite a generated world mid-session, with sound

DeepMind's newest world model reaches Google AI Ultra subscribers inside Project Genie. Genie 4 takes new text prompts while you walk through a generated world, so you can change the weather, drop in a character or redirect the action, and it adds real-time audio that follows the scene, from ambience to dialogue. Genie 3 stays in the model picker with upgrades of its own: up to 3 minutes per generation (from 60 seconds), linked worlds, and worlds seeded from 360-degree panoramas. A Gemini-driven agent builds worlds from a conversation and can trigger events while you explore. This is a product post with no benchmarks, but it is a usable look at how promptable, controllable world simulation now behaves.

WHY: World models · promptable simulation · generative audio · interactive environments
04✦AI ModelsHugging Face

Streaming MoE experts from SSD runs a 180B model on a 32 GB Mac within 6% of Strata

Hugging Face's Tarek Ziadé made Transformers run Qwen3.8-Flash-Next (48 layers of 512 experts, 10 active, 121 GB in FP8) on a 32 GB M5 MacBook. The key trick is a slot pool: one preallocated device tensor shared by all layers, with router expert ids remapped to slots, so the stock grouped_mm kernel runs on a 12 GiB LRU cache as if it were a normal expert bank. Experts are quantized to 3 bits (49 GB on disk), eight I/O threads read misses, and the next two layers' routers run early to prefetch. Final numbers: 13.9 tok/s decode against Strata's 14.8, and 228 tok/s prefill against 220. The remaining gap is host round trips that PyTorch's MPS stream can't yet hide. The option is in an open Transformers PR, #50717.

Hugging Face ↗Nov 18Primary
WHY: Local inference · mixture-of-experts offloading · Apple silicon · open-source infrastructure
05⌬RoboticsRhoda AI

Rhoda AI pretrains its robot policy on “hundreds of years” of ordinary video

Out of stealth this year with a $450 million Series A, Palo Alto's Rhoda AI deploys a direct video action (DVA) model: from a history of video frames, action tokens and language tokens it predicts the next half second of video, an action converter turns that prediction into robot commands, and after each move the loop observes and predicts again. CEO Jagdeep Singh says pretraining used hundreds of years of video, not only egocentric manipulation, and that models trained on the broadest data, baseball and crashing waves included, performed best. Ten to 20 hours of task data then adapts it to a workflow, and the robot body only enters at post-training. A mobile DVA folds navigation into the same policy. The claims are the company's.

The Robot Report ↗Nov 13Brief
WHY: Video world models · robot pretraining · cross-embodiment policies · industrial deployment
06⌬RoboticsSkydio

Skydio's F10 Lightrunner puts its camera on one wingtip and flies lopsided on purpose

Fixed-wing drones that circle a target keep hiding it behind a wing or tail, so Skydio put the camera on a wingtip: as long as the aircraft only turns toward that side, the view stays clear. The camera is a large share of the drag, so a counterweight on the other tip would have nearly halved flight time; instead the camera-side wing is longer for extra lift and the two motors sit asymmetrically to balance thrust. One model-predictive-control policy covers hover to 100 mph with no mode switch, which lets the MegaDock's robot arm catch, recharge and relaunch up to five F10s, three flying while two charge. Flight time is up to 2 hours over dual 5G links, with a parachute for failures.

IEEE Spectrum ↗Nov 15Analysis
WHY: Aerial robotics · asymmetric aircraft design · model predictive control · autonomous docking
07⌬RoboticsNVIDIA

NVIDIA's SimReady walkthrough has an agent build a validated pick-and-place scene in Isaac Sim

A hands-on guide: download a UR17G arm, a Robotiq 2F-140 gripper and a beverage can from SimReady Central, the open OpenUSD asset library on Hugging Face, each shipped with authored collisions, mass, joints and drives plus a validation report (Robot-Body 4.0.0 and Robot-Gripper 3.0.0 profiles, passing PhysX driven-joint checks). Then start Isaac Sim 6.2 with its Python server on and let an agent with the Isaac Sim skills (the post uses GPT-6 Sol) assemble the robot, test joints, motion and contact, and write the controls that grasp, lift, move and release the can. The useful warning: a robot can look right standing still and misbehave once physics runs, so test before you trust the scene.

NVIDIA · Hugging Face ↗Nov 13Primary
WHY: Robot simulation · OpenUSD assets · agentic tooling · hands-on guide
08✦AI Modelsggml-org

llama.cpp and ggml reach v1.0.0 and promise semantic versioning for their C APIs

Three and a half years in, ggml-org tagged libllama and libggml 1.0.0. From now on, a breaking change to the public C headers in ./include bumps the major version; backend updates, new model support and API additions are minor releases, and bug fixes are patches. Builds ship as pre-release artifacts for Linux, macOS, Windows, Android and iOS, and through Docker, Debian, Homebrew, conda-forge, MacPorts, Winget and an XCFramework. The project supports more than 150 architecture variants and the mainstream speculative-decoding methods, with CI on self-hosted runners from NVIDIA, AMD, Qualcomm, Arm, IBM and Intel. Apps built on it can now pin a version and know what an upgrade means.

ggml-org · Hugging Face ↗Nov 13Primary
WHY: Local inference · open-source infrastructure · API stability · quantization
09⌬RoboticsAi2

PINNED

Ai2's ToyBlocks gives robot learning a controlled world where failures can be traced

The first of Ai2's “FoundationWorlds” is an open simulation of an arm and lettered blocks that auto-generates RL and motion-planned demonstrations in Isaac Lab, with no teleoperation, and lets researchers vary data and hold tasks out. Early findings: ManiFlow, π0.5 and MolmoAct 2 generalize poorly to new block positions and stack heights; a Claude Opus 5.5 agent solved 30 of 30 classic stacking plans but only 63% of search-and-spell tasks, mostly from letter confusions and wrong orientations. Code, data and models are public.

Ai2 · Hugging Face ↗Nov 6Primary
WHY: Robot learning testbed · VLA generalization · open simulation · agentic manipulation
010⌬RoboticsNVIDIA

PINNED

NVIDIA's Cosmos Framework adds runnable recipes for simulated camera and LiDAR worlds

The open framework now ships agent-runnable recipes and released Cosmos 3 Nano checkpoints that generate synchronized camera video and LiDAR for driving, robot and surgical scenarios, and it extends to GR00T, Alpamayo, pi0.5 and other compatible models. It's a practical guide: Nano generation needs 58 GiB in BF16 or 44 GiB in FP8 with offloading, and NVIDIA reports roughly 2–3× speedups from speculative decoding on H200.

WHY: World models · synchronized sensor simulation · open recipes · physical AI
